ST. MARTIN'S HIGH SCHOOL - TEACHER INTEGRATION PATCH

Files changed:
1. server.js
   - Teacher announcement API accepts a page destination (News & Updates or Resources).
   - Teacher image uploads accept a destination (News & Updates or School Gallery).
   - Public News and Resources routes show approved posts for their own page destination.
2. views/teacher-dashboard.ejs
   - Announcement and calendar forms submit to the authenticated JSON APIs.
   - Image upload form sends multipart data to the authenticated upload API.
   - Teacher can choose whether an uploaded image should appear on News & Updates or School Gallery.

Publishing workflow:
- Teacher submits an announcement, calendar event, or image.
- Submission stays pending until the principal approves it.
- Approved announcements appear on their selected public page.
- Approved images appear on the selected News & Updates or School Gallery page.

Password note:
- Teacher passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes and cannot be displayed/recovered as plaintext.
- The Principal Dashboard includes a Reset Password action; use that to set a new password securely.
